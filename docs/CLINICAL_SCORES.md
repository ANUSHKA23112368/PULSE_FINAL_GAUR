# Clinical Risk Assessment Scores in PULSE

PULSE integrates standardized physiological deterioration and risk assessment algorithms:

1. **Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS)**: 3–15 scale assessing eye opening, verbal response, and motor function.
2. **National Early Warning Score 2 (NEWS2)**: Aggregates respiration rate, oxygen saturation, systolic BP, pulse, consciousness, and temperature.
3. **Braden Scale for Pressure Sore Risk**: 6 subscales evaluating sensory perception, moisture, activity, mobility, nutrition, and friction.
4. **Morse Fall Scale (MFS)**: Rapid clinical assessment of inpatient fall risk.
