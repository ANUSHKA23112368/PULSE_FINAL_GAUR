### 1.2 Core Academic Literature Cited & Theoretical Foundations
The PULSE system grounds its design on six primary academic and clinical publications:
- **Suominen et al. (2015)** (*NICTA & Australian National University*): "Comprehensive dataset of synthetic nursing handover reports for natural language processing research." Provides the 101 synthetic nursing handover reports (`data/101writtenfreetextreports/`) and CRF feature schemas (`data/101informationextraction/`).
- **Alsentzer et al. (2019)** (*Clinical NLP Workshop*): "Publicly available clinical BERT embeddings." Establishes the Bio_ClinicalBERT transformer architecture pre-trained on MIMIC-III notes, deployed in `backend/models/clinical_bert/`.
- **Harkema et al. (2009) / Chapman et al. (2001)** (*Journal of Biomedical Informatics*): Context/NegEx algorithm for clinical assertion and temporality classification, implemented in `backend/extraction/context_extractor.py`.
- **Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care (ACSQHC, 2019)**: National implementation toolkit for the ISBAR handover protocol (Identify, Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation), synthesized in `backend/extraction/structured_generator.py`.
- **Joint Commission on Accreditation of Healthcare Organizations (JCAHO, 2017)**: Sentinel Event Alert on inadequate hand-off communication, informing PULSE's clinical risk mitigation and human-review flags.
- **Feinstein & Cicchetti (1990)**: High agreement but low kappa paradox in clinical observation, governing the Inter-Rater Reliability specification in `docs/benchmark/annotation_agreement_protocol.md`.

## 3. Critical Architectural Insights from the Audit

1. **The Core Pipeline is Genuinely Implemented and Functioning**:
   The end-to-end execution path—from non-destructive preprocessing through shorthand normalizer, Hinglish mapper, entity extractor, ConText assertion/temporality analyzer, structured handover generator, SQLite persistence, and React frontend—is **fully implemented and verified by 167 passing tests**.
2. **Key Clinical Gaps Identified**:
   - **Medication PRN Indications**: The schema and benchmark fixtures define `prn_indication`, but the live rule extractor in `entity_extractor.py` does not currently extract or bind PRN indications to the medication entity.
   - **Investigation Status Enum Drift**: The live extractor uses `"Pending"`, `"Unspecified"`, and `"Not Ordered / Denied"`, whereas the benchmark schema mandates `"Ordered"`, `"Pending"`, `"Sent"`, `"Awaiting Results"`, `"Completed"`, `"Unknown"`.
   - **Conditional Instructions**: Handled as general pending tasks; lacking a dedicated `(trigger_condition, contingent_action)` relational structure.
   - **Missing Attributes Representation**: The live extractor uses placeholder strings (`"Unspecified dose in text"`) rather than `None`/`null` internally.
3. **Model Realism**:
   PULSE correctly avoids exaggerating model capabilities: Bio_ClinicalBERT is acknowledged as an uncalibrated experimental baseline (Strict F1 11.38%), while the Hybrid Pipeline serves as the primary high-precision extraction engine (Strict F1 47.42%, Lenient F1 68.04%, Med Attribute Binding 90.5%).
